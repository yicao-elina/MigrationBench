# Figure captions
Captions are also embedded beside their corresponding figures in the manuscript and SI.

## Figure 1

**A material contains several distinct tests of an interatomic potential.** **a,** Cr-doped Sb₂Te₃, showing the Te--Sb--Te--Sb--Te quintuple layers (QLs), the van der Waals gap and representative dopant displacements. Te, Sb and Cr are pink, cyan and yellow, respectively. **b,** SOAP-based principal-component visualization of reference configurations, thermal trajectories, migration images and layer-sliding configurations. Symbols distinguish configuration sources and reference split assignments; the projection is a descriptive view of sampled configurations, not a calibrated measure of extrapolation risk. **c--h,** Local views of static configurations, molecular dynamics, in-gap migration, gap-to-QL motion, within-QL motion and sliding, in that order. The task categories organize the tests; their ordering does not define a universal or quantitatively monotonic distribution-shift scale.

## Figure 2

**Training and held-out pointwise agreement across four model conditions.** **a--d,** Columns show Scratch-600K, FT-600K, Scratch-MultiT and FT-MultiT, respectively. Rows show training energies, test energies, training force components and test force components. Energies are per atom and are centred by subtracting the pooled DFT mean for the corresponding trajectory cluster from both DFT and MLIP values; test-only cluster means are used for visualization only. Forces are uncentred Cartesian components. Predictions from three training seeds are pooled, so the displayed $n$ counts prediction records rather than independent structures: 5,358/801 training/test energy records for each 600 K family and 7,977/2,301 for each MultiT family. Hexbin colour denotes count on logarithmic scales, with separate normalizations for energy and force. Dashed lines indicate identity; $\rho$ and $r$ are Spearman and Pearson correlations. The force display is restricted to $±10$ eV Å$^{-1}$, leaving 106 Scratch-MultiT test components outside the plotted window; those components remain in all numerical metrics. Uncentred, seed-resolved errors are given in Supplementary Table S1.

## Figure 3

**Molecular dynamics tests the configurations generated by each model.** The comparison contains MPA-0, MP-0b3 and OMAT-0 foundation checkpoints and seeds 123, 234 and 345 for FT-600K, FT-MultiT, Scratch-600K and Scratch-MultiT. **a,** Kinetic temperature. **b,** Hydrostatic pressure, $P=-tr(\sigma)/3$, from archived stress tensors. **c,** In-plane, multiple-origin MSD with triclinic periodic unwrapping. **d,** Pair-resolved coordination numbers, using fixed Cr--Sb, Cr--Te and Sb--Te cutoffs of 3.2, 4.1 and 4.2 Å, respectively; bars and whiskers summarize the mean and sample standard deviation within each displayed condition. **e,** All-atom RDFs from ordered pair counts and triclinic minimum-image distances; thin traces retain individual models and the summaries use display-only smoothing. **f,** Normalized multiple-origin velocity autocorrelation. Insets expand early-time behaviour. Colours distinguish strategies, open/filled symbols distinguish 600 K/MultiT training, and markers identify training seeds; foundation differences are checkpoint variation. Runs start at a common 200 ps state and target 1,000 ps, using a 1 fs timestep and 200-step output interval. Curves end at their observed horizons; structural summaries use the available, unequal trajectory windows. Only three foundation checkpoints and FT-600K seed 234 reach the target (Supplementary Table S8).

## Figure 4

**Fixed geometries and model-relaxed paths reveal different discrepancies.** **a--c,** Energy profiles evaluated on fixed reference images for in-gap, gap-to-QL and within-QL motion, respectively. **d--f,** Corresponding endpoint-initialized, MLIP-relaxed pathway diagnostics. Energies are relative to each profile's first image; the horizontal coordinate is normalized image index and does not imply that optimized paths follow the same geometric arc. Dashed dark curves are the DFT reference profiles. Coloured curves and bands summarize the archived model groups and their pointwise variation. The scratch curve is explicitly the legacy scratch condition, rather than the complete contemporary Scratch-600K/MultiT seed matrix in Fig. 5; ensemble membership is documented in Supplementary Table S3. Missing model profiles indicate unavailable or excluded accepted records. The reference peaks are 1.5243, 0 and 1.8449 eV for the three columns. Their source records retain SCF warnings, so they are image-set energy diagnostics. Self-relaxed profiles are endpoint-only MLIP NEB proxies and do not establish DFT-validated minimum-energy paths.

## Figure 5

**Reliability depends on the task and evaluation operator.** **a,** Evaluation design: three foundation checkpoints and three training seeds for each of two fine-tuned and two scratch conditions, evaluated over three fixed migration paths, three self-relaxed migration paths and two fixed sliding paths. **b,** The 120-record source matrix. Colour encodes the predicted forward peak relative to the first image; circle area follows a square-root mapping of the absolute discrepancy from the reference. Colour uses a power normalization with exponent 0.40. A minimum dot remains at zero discrepancy. **c,d,** Within-QL forward peaks for fixed and self-relaxed paths; the reference line is 1.8449 eV and the vertical scales differ. **e,** Fixed-path sliding peaks for pristine and Cr-in-gap systems, with reference peaks of 1.6538 and 2.5708 eV. Shapes identify training seeds or foundation checkpoints. All finite values are retained as diagnostics. Crosses identify five nonconverged records; the remaining source tags comprise 75 finite-diagnostic and 40 optimizer-converged records. A hash-matched raw-record correction changes the Scratch-600K seed-123 within-QL self peak from 0 to 2.351 eV and corrects two convergence labels (Supplementary Sec. 2.2). Neither a small dot nor a small self-relaxed peak certifies a physical barrier. Migration references retain SCF warnings, and fixed sliding endpoints and profiles require separate consistency checks. Numerical values, statuses and the correction audit accompany the figure.

## Figure 6

**Trajectory-feature projections across the 15-model MD comparison.** **a,** $t$-SNE with perplexity 30 and random state 42. **b,** PHATE with random state 42. **c,** UMAP with 15 neighbours, minimum distance 0.1 and random state 42; colour intensity indicates local two-dimensional bin count on a 45-bin histogram, not energy or physical probability. **d,** Per-condition means of model-specific silhouette scores computed in the $t$-SNE plane, with individual checkpoint/seed symbols and a dashed overall value of 0.117. These are projection-space scores, not original-space separability or independent thermal-replica uncertainty. The input contains 927 frames, with 4--101 frames per model, and combines energies, Cartesian forces, neighbour histograms and per-trajectory SOAP/PCA summaries. Model energy offsets, feature scale, separate SOAP PCA bases and unequal failure horizons limit geometric interpretation. Original-space and projection-sensitivity checks on the same dataset are reported in Supplementary Fig. S12 and Supplementary Table S9. Constant columns are excluded from the quantitative distance analysis.

## Figure 7

**Structural predictors of energy error depend on model condition and training seed.** TreeSHAP explains gradient-boosted surrogates of the absolute energy error per atom on one common 267-frame test trajectory. **a,** Mean absolute SHAP values for the 20 features selected by the shared global ranking, with mean and sample standard deviation over training seeds 123, 234 and 345 within each of four conditions. Strategy colour distinguishes fine-tuning and scratch training; open/filled bars distinguish 600 K and MultiT. **b,** The same 20 feature rows aligned across all 12 model--seed columns on a shared meV atom$^{-1}$ scale. Species pairs and $n_1n_2,l$ identify SOAP power-spectrum channels; they are descriptor indices, not isolated bonds or MACE neurons. Surrogates use 407 computed features and frame-aligned errors. Fidelity of the separate five-fold diagnostic estimator is given in Supplementary Table S11; it does not make the full-data TreeSHAP fit an out-of-sample causal explanation.

## Figure S1

**Training routes and energy prediction.** **a,** Schematic mapping from atomic coordinates through descriptors and a neural energy model to an extensive total energy, expressed as a sum of atomic contributions. **b--d,** Foundation pretraining on a broad corpus, specialist training from random initialization on target data, and adaptation of a foundation checkpoint on target data. ## Figure S8

**Why the two original NEB evaluations were not comparable.** **a,** Fixed-image energy assignments for the 61-atom, one-Cr G1 set, with its 0.336 eV QE reference. **b,** Archived model-driven optimization behaviour, illustrating that the band can become unstable when forces move the images. ## Figure S9

**Fixed-path foundation-model diagnostics for four dopants in Sb₂Te₃.** Rows show Cr (**a--c**), Cu (**d--f**), Li (**g--i**) and Na (**j--l**); columns show in-gap, gap-to-QL and within-QL configurations. Curves compare the displayed MACE-MP, MACE-OMAT-0 and MACE-MH checkpoint groups with the screened QE path table. Energies are relative to the initial image, and the horizontal coordinate is normalized image index. Group bands describe available checkpoint variation. A downhill reference can have a zero forward peak while retaining a nonzero reverse peak or endpoint difference. The QE records retain convergence limitations, so the curves are diagnostic energy assignments rather than validated activation energies. They do not establish the cross-material generality of the main model-selection conclusions.

## Figure S10

**Sliding profiles across foundation, fine-tuned and scratch routes.** **a,b,** Fixed-path relative energies for pristine and Cr-in-gap Sb₂Te₃, respectively. **c,d,** Endpoint-initialized self-relaxed profiles for the same system categories. Solid coloured summaries distinguish the displayed training conditions and their available member variation; the dashed dark curve is the supplied DFT reference. Peak agreement, profile agreement, endpoint return and self-relaxation convergence are separate requirements. ## Figure S11
