## 2. How the data is aggregated

1. **Survey → sources.** Surveys of the repo, the ChatGPT workspace, ~/Downloads, Remotion and Rockfish are recorded in `sources/*.tsv` (one row per panel/table, per Rockfish directory, per checkpoint). Fixes made after the survey go in `sources/status_overrides.tsv`.
2. **Import.** `tools/import_external.py` copies what the trace needs into the repo per `imports/import_plan.tsv`; heavy binaries go to the archive store. Every file is logged with sha256.
3. **Build.** `tools/build_index.py` joins sources, overrides and manifests into the master TSVs, the per-item `PROVENANCE.md` pages and migration `002` of the SQL registry. All paths are written portably (table below).
4. **Legacy registry.** `tools/clean_legacy_registry.py` (migration `003`) makes the 2026-10-25 registry tables portable too.
5. **Check.** `tools/verify_trace.py` (paths, sha256), `tools/check_sql.py` (integrity, keys, row counts), `tools/verify_table_rounding.py` (hand-rounded tables), `tests/`.
