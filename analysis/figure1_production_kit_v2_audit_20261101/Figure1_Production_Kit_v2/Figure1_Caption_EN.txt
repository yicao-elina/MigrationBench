Figure 1 | A task-conditioned design for comparing initialization and thermal target-data coverage. a, Schematic Cr-doped Sb2Te3, with Te, Sb and Cr represented in pink, cyan and gold. QL denotes a quintuple layer, with adjacent QLs separated by a vdW gap. The atomistic illustrations are visual references for later coordinate-based rendering, not optimized transition structures. b, The fitted comparison crosses random or foundation initialization with 600 K or multi-temperature target data. S600/SMT denote scratch models and FT600/FTMT foundation-initialized fine-tuning under the two regimes. Three symbols in each cell identify training seeds 123 (circle), 234 (triangle) and 345 (square); open/filled symbols denote 600 K/multi-temperature training. An independent zero-shot branch uses foundation weights without target fitting. All four fitted cells and the zero-shot branch return the same energy/force observables; matching of capacity and optimization budget is not implied by this schematic. c, Distinct probes measure pointwise residuals, dynamical rollout, local in-gap motion, entry and intralayer environments, and collective sliding. Their ordering is not a calibrated OOD coordinate. d, Fixed-image scoring and model-relaxed evaluation use distinct configuration operators. The drawn image paths are conceptual, not energy profiles. e, Cr displacements calculated from ten supplied images per input route after consecutive minimum-image unwrapping. The normal is defined by the first two cell vectors; lateral displacement is the norm of the orthogonal component. The short nominal entry input remains near the origin; its expanded view is supplied separately. These coordinates are not joined to the energy table and do not establish penetration, reference convergence or training-set coverage.
