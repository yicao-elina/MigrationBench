## Results and discussion
The last-table stability screen retained eight of ten candidate paths. The retained paths span a maximum penultimate-to-final image energy change of 0.000491–0.050366 eV, whereas the two rejected paths exhibit 0.154644 and 19.062111 eV. This separation motivates using the retained paths as exploratory fixed-geometry energy samples while keeping the rejected paths outside the model-comparison reference set.

After the Hartree-to-eV correction, the retained base paths give median relative-profile MAEs of 0.0606 eV for Foundation and 0.1154 eV for MPA0, with median absolute barrier-like discrepancies of 0.0541 and 0.1418 eV, respectively. Agreement remains strongly path dependent: the Cu neb-QLs profile is an outlier for both models, while the gap2QL traces are comparatively consistent. Foundation barrier-like errors on the three gap2QL paths are 0.0018–0.0549 eV in absolute value; the corresponding MPA0 range is 0.0741–0.1768 eV. The pattern is therefore more informative as a stress test of model response and path-local extrapolation than as a ranking of DFT fidelity.

The imposed perturbations reveal an additional sensitivity axis. Median absolute responses remain small on most paths but become large on the same high-discrepancy trajectories, indicating that local perturbation response can identify unstable or out-of-distribution regions. Because the underlying images are not demonstrated equilibrium structures and the perturbation amplitudes are finite, these results should not be interpreted as harmonic force constants or equilibrium phonon response.

Finally, FT-600K was not silently treated as a scientific failure: all 240 evaluations failed at model input validation because the deployed model lacks Cu, Li, and Na species support. This is a package/model-coverage blocker and should be reported separately from energetic disagreement.
