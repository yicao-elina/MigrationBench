# Results draft (evidence-bounded)

中文：10 条候选 DFT NEB 路径均已完成只读 provenance 审计，但没有路径满足最终所有 image 的显式 NEB 收敛、有限 energy/error、identity 与 cell/PBC 门槛。因此本阶段不报告 Foundation MACE 的路径精度或扰动稳定性。

English: All 10 candidate DFT NEB paths were provenance-audited read-only, but none passed the final explicit NEB convergence, finite image energy/error, identity, and cell/PBC gates. Foundation MACE pathway accuracy and perturbation stability are therefore not reported in this stage.
