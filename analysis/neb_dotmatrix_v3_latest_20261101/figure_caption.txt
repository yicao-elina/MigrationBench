NEB evaluation with a dual-encoded dot matrix. (a) All foundation checkpoints and trained models enter all evaluation blocks. Foundation comprises three checkpoints; fine-tuning and scratch each comprise two training regimes with three seeds per regime. (b) Each matrix position represents one checkpoint or training seed and one pathway or system. Circle fill encodes the supplied predicted barrier or peak energy in eV using a single global 0-27 eV blue scale with PowerNorm gamma=0.40. Circle area encodes absolute error in eV using a square-root area scale with a visible minimum: s = s_min + (s_max - s_min) sqrt(error / 25 eV). Here s is the scatter area parameter in pt^2, proportional to geometric circle area. Minimum and maximum marker diameters equal 0.20 and 0.82 times the matrix row pitch, respectively; zero error retains the minimum visible dot. Size legend circles are generated by the identical mapping used for matrix circles. All 120 supplied finite observations are rendered as quantitative product metrics; the original run status is retained only in plotted_values.tsv and audit.json for provenance. No energies or errors are clipped. (c,d) Within-QL predicted barriers for fixed-path and self-relaxed evaluations. (e) Sliding peak energies. Colors in a, c, d and e identify training strategy; shape in quantitative supporting panels identifies seed or checkpoint. Every dot-matrix observation uses a circle. Dashed lines mark the canonical DFT references. Points are horizontally separated for visibility; within trained strategies in c and d, 600K seeds appear left and MultiT seeds right. Panels c, d and e use different linear y scales. For migration, error is the absolute difference between model and canonical DFT forward barriers. For sliding, it is the absolute difference between model and DFT relative-energy maxima. Fixed-path migration and sliding are supporting diagnostics according to the supplied metadata; all supplied finite values are shown directly as product metrics. No confidence intervals or significance tests are inferred.
