# 15-model MD figure R03 change note

R03 changes only panel F of the R02 figure: F is restored to the original normalized VACF panel. Panels A–E, the raw trajectory analysis, 15-model matrix, palette, and status classification are unchanged.

The model matrix remains 15/15 with four target-reaching trajectories (three Foundation plus FT600K seed234) and eleven scientific-threshold failures. All 15 model-specific series, MSD, VACF, and RDF outputs have `invalid_frames=0` and finite core temperature/pressure metrics. Scratch curves remain visible in the orange/yellow palette and are plotted only through their observed ~200–204 ps horizons.

The RDF 0–2 Å correction from R02 is retained: zero bins are kept and plotted on a zero-inclusive symlog ordinate rather than discarded by a logarithmic transform.
