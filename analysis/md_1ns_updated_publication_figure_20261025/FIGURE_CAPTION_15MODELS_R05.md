# Updated 1 ns MD figure — 15-model R05 release

Evidence-bounded comparison of the complete 15-model MD matrix: three zero-shot MACE Foundation checkpoints (MPA-0, MP-0b3 and OMAT-0), three grouped-A2 FT-600K seeds, three grouped-A2 FT-MultiT seeds, three Scratch-Lmax1 600K seeds and three Scratch-Lmax1 MultiT seeds. (A) Instantaneous kinetic temperature from raw extxyz momenta. (B) Hydrostatic pressure, $P=-\mathrm{tr}(\sigma)/3$, from the raw extxyz stress tensor. (C) In-plane mean-square displacement with triclinic fractional unwrapping. (D) Pair-resolved local coordination numbers from the raw Rockfish trajectories: Cr–Sb, Cr–Te and Sb–Te. The three pair-specific cutoffs are fixed from the first minima of the pooled stable-reference partial RDFs: 3.2, 4.1 and 4.2 Å, respectively. Bars show the model-class mean and error bars show the sample standard deviation across model-internal points. The four clusters are Foundation (three Foundation checkpoints), FT-600K (three seeds), FT-MultiT (three seeds) and Scratch (the six 600K/MultiT Scratch seeds pooled as the requested fourth class). (E) Cr–Cr radial distribution function from raw Rockfish trajectories; the zero-inclusive symmetric-log ordinate retains the physical $g(r)=0$ baseline, including 0–2 Å. (F) Normalized multi-origin velocity autocorrelation function.

All 15 model-specific trajectories remain in the source data and audit table; only panel D is aggregated for readability. Foundation-MPA-0, Foundation-MP-0b3, Foundation-OMAT-0 and FT600K-A2 seed234 reach the 1 ns target; the remaining trajectories are retained as truncated scientific-threshold/failure-mode evidence and are not extended to 1 ns. The R05 change is limited to replacing the R04 per-model short-range Cr–Cr CN point panel with the three-pair, four-cluster mean±SD summary; panels A–C, E and F retain the R04 data, styling and palette.
