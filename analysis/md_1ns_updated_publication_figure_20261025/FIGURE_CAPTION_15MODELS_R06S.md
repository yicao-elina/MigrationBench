# Updated 1 ns MD figure — 15-model R06S release

Evidence-bounded comparison of the complete 15-model MD matrix: three zero-shot MACE Foundation checkpoints (MPA-0, MP-0b3 and OMAT-0), three grouped-A2 FT-600K seeds, three grouped-A2 FT-MultiT seeds, three Scratch-Lmax1 600K seeds and three Scratch-Lmax1 MultiT seeds. (A) Instantaneous kinetic temperature from raw extxyz momenta. (B) Hydrostatic pressure, $P=-\mathrm{tr}(\sigma)/3$, from the raw extxyz stress tensor. (C) In-plane mean-square displacement with triclinic fractional unwrapping. (D) Pair-resolved local coordination numbers from the raw Rockfish trajectories: Cr–Sb, Cr–Te and Sb–Te. The three pair-specific cutoffs are fixed from the first minima of pooled stable-reference partial RDFs: 3.2, 4.1 and 4.2 Å, respectively. Bars show model-class mean ± sample standard deviation across model-internal points. Foundation, FT-600K and FT-MultiT contain three points each; Scratch pools the six Scratch-600K/MultiT seeds into the requested fourth class. (E) Cr–Cr radial distribution function from the raw Rockfish trajectories, displayed after a conservative Gaussian smoothing for readability (sigma = 1.15 bins, approximately 0.115 Å at $dr=0.1$ Å). The raw RDF values and the zero-inclusive baseline, including the 0–2 Å region, are retained in the source data. (F) Normalized multi-origin velocity autocorrelation function. A/B/C/F contain compact early-time zoom insets using the same 15 model curves and palette; A/B/C are placed lower-right to avoid the main trace band, while F remains upper-right as in the reference. The windows are 190–270 ps for A/B, 0–80 ps for C and 0–8 ps for F.

All 15 model-specific trajectories remain in the source data and audit table; only panel D is aggregated for readability and panel E is display-smoothed. Foundation-MPA-0, Foundation-MP-0b3, Foundation-OMAT-0 and FT600K-A2 seed234 reach the 1 ns target; the remaining trajectories are retained as truncated scientific-threshold/failure-mode evidence and are not extended to 1 ns. R06S changes panel E only relative to R05; panels A–D and F retain the R05 data, styling and palette.
