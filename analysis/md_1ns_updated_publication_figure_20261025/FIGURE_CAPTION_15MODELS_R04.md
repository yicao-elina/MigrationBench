# Updated 1 ns MD figure — 15-model R04 release

Evidence-bounded comparison of the complete 15-model MD matrix: three zero-shot MACE Foundation checkpoints (MPA-0, MP-0b3 and OMAT-0), three grouped-A2 FT-600K seeds, three grouped-A2 FT-MultiT seeds, three Scratch-Lmax1 600K seeds and three Scratch-Lmax1 MultiT seeds. (A) Instantaneous kinetic temperature from raw extxyz momenta. (B) Hydrostatic pressure, $P=-\mathrm{tr}(\sigma)/3$, from the raw extxyz stress tensor. (C) In-plane mean-square displacement with triclinic fractional unwrapping. (D) Short-range Cr–Cr coordination/contact number, $N_{\mathrm{Cr-Cr}}(r<2.5\,\AA)$, obtained by integrating the validated Cr–Cr RDF with the common Cr number density; each point is one of the 15 model trajectories. This fixed short-range metric is intended to expose anomalously close Cr–Cr contacts in truncated/failure-mode trajectories, not to claim a complete first-shell coordination number. (E) Cr–Cr radial distribution function from raw Rockfish trajectories; the zero-inclusive symmetric-log ordinate retains the physical $g(r)=0$ baseline, including 0–2 Å. (F) Normalized multi-origin velocity autocorrelation function.

All 15 model traces or markers are shown individually. Foundation-MPA-0, Foundation-MP-0b3, Foundation-OMAT-0 and FT600K-A2 seed234 reach the 1 ns target; the remaining trajectories are retained as truncated scientific-threshold/failure-mode evidence and are not extended to 1 ns. The R04 change is limited to replacing the duplicated D VACF panel with the RDF-derived short-range Cr–Cr CN/contact metric; panels A–C, E and F retain the R03 data, styling and palette.
