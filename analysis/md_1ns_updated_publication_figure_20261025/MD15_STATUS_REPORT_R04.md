# 15-model MD figure R04 change note

R04 changes only panel D of R03: D is now the short-range Cr–Cr coordination/contact number integrated from the validated Cr–Cr RDF to a fixed cutoff of 2.5 Å. The metric is reported in neighbors per Cr and is plotted as one marker for each of the 15 models. It is explicitly interpreted as a short-range contact/failure-mode diagnostic rather than a complete first-shell coordination number.

Panels A–C, E and F, the raw trajectory analysis, 15-model matrix, status classification, and palette are unchanged. The 15-model matrix remains four target-reaching trajectories (three Foundation plus FT600K seed234) and eleven scientific-threshold failures. All 15 model-specific series, MSD, VACF, and RDF outputs have `invalid_frames=0` and finite core temperature/pressure metrics. Scratch curves remain visible in the orange/yellow palette and are plotted only through their observed ~200–204 ps horizons.

The RDF 0–2 Å correction from R02/R03 is retained: zero bins are kept and plotted on a zero-inclusive symlog ordinate rather than discarded by a logarithmic transform.
