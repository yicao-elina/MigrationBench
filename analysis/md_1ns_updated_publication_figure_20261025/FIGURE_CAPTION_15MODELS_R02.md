# Updated 1 ns MD figure — 15-model release

**Figure.** Evidence-bounded comparison of the complete 15-model MD matrix: three zero-shot MACE Foundation checkpoints (MPA-0, MP-0b3 and OMAT-0), three grouped-A2 FT-600K seeds, three grouped-A2 FT-MultiT seeds, three Scratch-Lmax1 600K seeds and three Scratch-Lmax1 MultiT seeds. (A) Instantaneous kinetic temperature recomputed from the per-atom momenta in the raw extxyz trajectory. (B) Hydrostatic pressure, $P=-\mathrm{tr}(\sigma)/3$, from the raw extxyz stress tensor. (C) Per-model in-plane mean-square displacement from the first retained production frame with triclinic fractional unwrapping. (D) Normalized multi-origin velocity autocorrelation function. (E) Cr–Cr radial distribution function calculated from the raw Rockfish trajectories with triclinic minimum-image distances. The ordinate is a zero-inclusive symmetric-log scale, so the physically empty short-range baseline, including 0–2 Å, is retained as $g(r)=0$ rather than removed by a logarithmic transform. (F) Ratio of the late to early $D_{xy}$ windows; a colored × denotes that the two-window estimate is not scientifically defined because the trajectory failed before the required window.

All 15 model traces are drawn individually. A trace is shown only through the last observed frame; the five grouped-A2 FT failures and six Scratch failure-mode runs are therefore visibly truncated, while Foundation-MPA-0, Foundation-MP-0b3, Foundation-OMAT-0 and FT600K-A2 seed234 reach the 1 ns target in the current evidence package. Failure-mode traces are retained for diagnosis and are not promoted to 1 ns transport or stability claims.

The model registry, current Rockfish status/ledger audit, raw trajectory manifest, per-model analysis CSVs and figure hashes are retained beside the editable renderer in this directory.
