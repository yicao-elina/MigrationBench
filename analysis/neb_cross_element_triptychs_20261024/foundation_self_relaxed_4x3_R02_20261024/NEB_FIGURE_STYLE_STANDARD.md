# NEB figure style standard

For subsequent NEB figures in this campaign and its release descendants:

- Use the exact x-axis label `Reaction Coordinate` in composite and standalone panels.
- Keep the x-axis title bold.
- Use visibly thick panel borders and a visibly thick dark dashed/open-diamond `DFT-NEB` reference line.
- Preserve the four-entry legend grammar: the model-family entries plus `DFT-NEB`; do not add `converged / included` or `failure / excluded` as visual legend items.
- Keep the full QE/DFT SCF-warning and fixed-path diagnostic boundary in the caption and manifest even when the legend uses the compact `DFT-NEB` label.
