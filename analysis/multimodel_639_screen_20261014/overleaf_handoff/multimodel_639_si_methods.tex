\paragraph{Failure-attribution candidate selection.}
The candidate pool was inherited without modification from the audited from-scratch instability campaign. It contains 639 unique source snapshots from three source conditions (`original`, `reproduce-1` and `reproduce-2`) and nine trajectory/seed branches. Candidates were selected by a stage-balanced policy spanning 207 early-stable controls, 216 stable pre-onset frames, 208 near-onset precursor frames, seven threshold/terminal frames and one 1,000-ps stable terminal target. This design enriches rare onset-related structures and is therefore not a time-weighted estimate of the molecular-dynamics state population.

\paragraph{Static MLIP evaluation.}
Each structure was evaluated without geometry relaxation or molecular-dynamics continuation using the canonical checkpoint paths and SHA256 values recorded in `MODEL_REGISTRY.tsv` and `MODEL_HASH_AUDIT.tsv`. The twelve-checkpoint roster comprised three from-scratch models, FT-600K and FT-MultiT checkpoints at training seeds 123, 234 and 345, and three foundation representatives (legacy compiled OMAT, MPA-0 medium and MP-0b3 medium). Energies and forces were computed in the Rockfish MACE 0.3.12 environment with ASE 3.25.0 and PyTorch 2.7.0+cu118. The production campaign used 32 chunks of at most 20 structures and completed with zero failed candidate--model evaluations.

\paragraph{Descriptor-space diagnostic.}
Structural novelty was assessed using the pre-existing Cr/Sb/Te SOAP descriptor (5~\AA cutoff, $\sigma=0.5$~\AA, $n_\mathrm{max}=4$, $l_\mathrm{max}=4$) and its 20-component PCA embedding. The training q95/q99 thresholds and candidate-to-reference distances are descriptive diagnostics; they are not calibrated uncertainty estimates and are not interpreted as causal evidence.
