\caption{\textbf{Diffusion-focused benchmarking of four MACE models in 1~ns molecular dynamics.} (B) Temperature trajectory at 600~K. (C) Maximum force along the trajectory, shown on a logarithmic scale to retain both bounded and runaway regimes. (D) Cr mean-squared displacement. (E) Pressure derived from the archived periodic-cell stress metadata. (F) Diffusion coefficients $D_{xy}$ from the 200--500~ps and 500--900~ps windows; diamonds denote full-window fits and open markers denote block fits. (G) Normalized velocity autocorrelation function. FT-600K and FT-MultiT are the stable trajectories used for diffusion reporting. Foundation-OMAT becomes unstable at 8.8~ps and From-scratch at 462.2~ps; these trajectories are retained as failure-mode benchmarks and are not assigned a $D_{xy}$. Heat-flux autocorrelation and thermal conductivity are intentionally not claimed because the original simulations did not record the per-frame heat current required for a Green--Kubo estimate.}
