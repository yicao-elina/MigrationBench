# Supplementary path-family audit

This folder preserves the path-classification evidence used to revise the migration discussion.

- `path_classification_61_81.csv` is copied from the 2026-10-03 independent Cr migration audit.
- `AUDIT_REPORT.md` is the accompanying provenance and quality report.

The audit distinguishes source-queue labels from geometry-supported classifications. The 61-atom paths 1--4 and 1--7 are in-gap candidates; 1--2 and 1--3 are deep-penetration candidates by the source-site definition; 1--5 requires a geometry recheck; 1--6 is mixed/ambiguous; and 1--8 is probable deep penetration but not a defensible three-bin match. The five 81-atom paths form a continuous deep-penetration chain, but their archived DFT outputs contain SCF warnings and are not manuscript-grade converged barriers. These records are therefore used to prevent unsupported pooled rankings, not to promote provisional values to final references.
