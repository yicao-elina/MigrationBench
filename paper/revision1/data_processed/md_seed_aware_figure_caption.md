# Seed-aware six-panel benchmark caption

**Figure. One-nanosecond MACE-MD stability, transport, and failure-mode diagnostics with seed-aware uncertainty.** (B) Temperature histories for the two fine-tuned families, shown as the median and 16--84% band across training seeds 123, 234, and 345; individual trajectories are retained as faint lines. The three foundation representatives are shown as dashed model-specific traces. From-scratch branches are marked by their scientifically observed failure events. (C) Maximum force on a logarithmic scale; the dashed line marks the 100 eV Å$^{-1}$ stability threshold. (D) MSD for stable FT seeds with the same empirical uncertainty band, together with the two stable non-OMAT foundation representatives. (E) Pressure for the archived parent four-model campaign; new seed/representative runs did not archive a stress tensor and are not assigned a pressure uncertainty. (F) $D_{xy}$ estimates from the 200--500 ps and 500--900 ps windows: circles and squares denote the two windows, individual FT seed estimates are shown explicitly, and diamonds with horizontal error bars are the mean ± sample SD across training seeds. Foundation triangles denote representative-model spread rather than a confidence interval. From-scratch is reported as *unstable — no $D_{xy}$ reported*. (G) Normalized VACF with seed-aware bands for the FT families. All MD trajectories use velocity seed 1100; uncertainty therefore reflects training-seed/model variation, not independent velocity replicas. HFACF and thermal conductivity are not reported because no heat-flux time series was archived.
