# FIG-004 results and discussion

The current FIG-004 release preserves the approved A--G page geometry and uses family means with uncertainty bands only where multiple provenance-matched stable training-seed trajectories are available. The two fine-tuned families therefore carry seed-level uncertainty in the time-course panels and per-seed estimates plus family summaries in panel F. The common MD velocity seed (1100) is recorded separately and is not treated as an independent replica dimension.

Failure-mode traces are retained before their first scientific threshold event (Foundation-OMAT-parent at 8.8 ps, From-scratch-legacy at 462.2 ps, from_scratch_all_data_v1-seed123 at 203.8 ps, from_scratch_all_data_v1-seed234 at 202.0 ps, from_scratch_all_data_v1-seed345 at 203.6 ps). Short pre-failure MSD fits are reported where position data exist, but they are not 1 ns transport coefficients and do not enter stable-family means. Panel C uses a logarithmic force axis. Panel E is populated only from real stress metadata and is omitted automatically if no finite pressure field is available. Panel G retains the long-time VACF and its 0--5 ps inset. No HFACF or thermal-conductivity claim is made because the required heat-flux time series is unavailable.

Exact model/input hashes, source paths, job identifiers, and unresolved historical provenance gaps are retained in MODEL_REGISTRY.tsv and the accompanying manifest. This release is publication-ready as a figure/evidence artifact only for rows marked verified by the acceptance gates; scheduler completion is not used as a substitute for scientific acceptance.
