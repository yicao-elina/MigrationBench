## 4. Bugs found in the published pipeline

**(a) SOAP label misalignment (affects every named feature).** Verified against
`dscribe/ext/soapGTO.cpp::getPD` (installed dscribe 2.1.2) and empirically:
dscribe lays out pair blocks in atomic-number order (Cr-Cr 0-49, Cr-Sb 50-129,
Cr-Te 130-209, Sb-Sb 210-259, Sb-Te 260-339, Te-Te 340-389) with **50 features for
homopairs** (n2>=n1 only) and 80 for heteropairs, and **l is the OUTERMOST** index.
`SOAPFeatureInterpreter` (`2-shap-plot-jhu.py:75-135`) assumes 80 features per pair with
l innermost and n1/n2 1-based over all 16 combinations. Result: **386 of 390 published
labels do not match the physical feature at that index** (asserted in `shap_audit.py`).
Consequences for the named claims: published `Cr-Cr_n13_l3` (index 13) is actually
`Cr-Cr_n14_l1`; published `Cr-Sb_n13_l0` (index 90) is `Cr-Sb_n31_l2`; published
`Cr-Cr_n22_l0` (index 25) is `Cr-Cr_n23_l2`; published `Cr-Cr_n42_l4` (index 69) is
`Cr-Sb_n14_l1`; published `Cr-Te_n33_l0` (index 210) is `Sb-Sb_n11_l0`.
(Note: the descriptor itself is invariant to input species *order* — verified
empirically — so the species-order variation across runs is harmless; the label map is not.)

**(b) Frame/feature misalignment in the JHU runs.** `create_model_datasets`
(`2-shap-plot-jhu.py:814-828`) subsamples errors to frames 0,5,10,...,145 but then indexes
the UNSAMPLED 150-row feature matrix with the subsample counter 0..29 — i.e. the error of
frame 5k is paired with the SOAP vector of frame k. SHAP attributions in Fig 6 couple 30
errors to the wrong 30 structures.

**(c) In-sample surrogate R².** Also explains R²=1.0000 for the 30-sample runs
(200-deep-tree GBR interpolates 30 points trivially). The honest question — "does SOAP
predict the model's error out-of-sample?" — is answered by the revised pipeline below.

**(d) No SHAP sampling seed** (`np.random.choice`, `2-shap-plot-jhu.py:393`) — harmless
here only because all 30 rows are selected anyway.

**(e) Foundation excluded silently.** Foundation's isolated-atom predictions are nonsense
(Te: -357.5 eV vs -2369.4 eV ground truth, `slurm-12324065.out:53-55`) => binding-energy
error 77029 eV (`:432`); the model was dropped from Fig 6 without this caveat appearing in
the paper.

## 5. Revised local regeneration

`Revision1/scripts/shap_fig6_regenerate.py` fixes (a)+(b)+(c) and recomputes everything
locally (150 frames is small). Outputs:
- `Revision1/data_processed/shap_surrogate_r2_revised.csv` — in-sample AND 5-fold-CV R² (seed 42)
- `Revision1/data_processed/shap_top_features_revised.csv` — top-50 per model with TRUE labels
- `Revision1/figures/fig6_shap_revised.pdf` — panels (a,b) per style spec.

Reproduction spot-check: FT-Multi-T total-energy MAE over 150 frames = 0.319546 eV,
exactly matching the original inference log (`0929-MultiT/slurm-12324065.out:426`) —
asserted in the script.

| Model | n | in-sample R² | 5-fold CV R² (seed 42) | 5-fold CV MAE (eV) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FT - 600K | 150 | 1.0000 | **0.9819** | 0.0380 |

=> The paper's "surrogate R²>0.7" claim survives honest held-out evaluation (all CV R² in

With correct frame alignment and true labels, the FT-Multi-T surrogate is dominated by
**Cr-Sb_n43_l0** (mean|SHAP| 0.076 eV) — NOT `Cr-Cr_n13_l3` as published. Its top-5:

Te-Te_n23_l4 (0.0083) — i.e. one dominant short-ranged spherical Cr-Sb descriptor and a
flat tail; the "sparse physically coherent representation" narrative qualitatively survives
(panel a of `figures/fig6_shap_revised.pdf`), but every specific feature name in §3.5 of the
paper must be replaced with the corrected pipeline's names
(`data_processed/shap_top_features_revised.csv`). Postscript: in the corrected pipeline,
`Cr-Sb_n13_l0` IS the rank-1 feature for FT-600K (0.026 eV) — the paper's "consensus"
name accidentally survived for one model, but its published justification (via scrambled
labels) was not valid; whether it is truly cross-model consensus must be re-stated from
`shap_top_features_revised.csv` (FT-Multi-T rank for it: see CSV; not rank 1 there).
