# Figure 2 seed-resolved nested boxplots

Train and held-out-test frame records are merged. Each model cluster contains three non-overlapping sub-boxplots, one for each seed; every sub-boxplot retains all finite frame-level scatter points for that seed. Boxes are computed in log10-RMSE coordinates and show the median, IQR, and 1.5-IQR whiskers. Black points and whiskers show the mean and sample SD of the three seed-level aggregate RMSE values. Significance brackets use paired two-sided t-tests on the three log10 seed-level aggregates and are exploratory because n=3; the retained comparisons are Scratch vs FT at 600 K, Scratch vs FT for MultiT, and FT 600 K vs FT MultiT. Cr classes are derived from n_atoms minus the 80-atom 2L bulk reference. No common 80-atom bulk/no-Cr trajectory is present, so the trajectory panel is excluded; O3 t1 is 84 atoms and is classified as Cr4.
