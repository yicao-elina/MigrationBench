Fig. 4b,c | Fixed-path relative-energy profiles for interlayer sliding in Sb2Te3. Black circles show the retained DFT reference from the ten-image no-CI Quantum ESPRESSO path; the reference remains classified as COMPLETED_BUT_SCF_WARNING. The current MACE evaluations use the same ten DFT geometries without relaxation. Colors follow the established FIG-168 mapping: Foundation (orange-red), FT-600K (dark blue), FT-MultiT (light blue), and from-scratch models (orange-yellow). Foundation checkpoint variants are shown individually and are not treated as a seed ensemble. FT-600K, FT-MultiT, Scratch-600K, and Scratch-MultiT show individual training-seed profiles together with mean +/- 1 SD bands where n=3; the two Scratch subfamilies are kept separate. These panels are fixed-path relative-energy diagnostics rather than self-relaxed or validated migration barriers. FR pseudopotential filenames are present in the retained DFT inputs, but explicit SOC flags were not independently confirmed.
